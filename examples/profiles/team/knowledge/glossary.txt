Glossary (Example) -- knowledge supports .txt files as well as .md.

Release: Shipping a new version to users. Scheduled on Thursday afternoons.
Hotfix: Urgent fix shipped outside the regular release window. Approved by ops (dave).
SEV1-SEV3: Incident severity levels (1 is highest). Criteria are in ops's escalation guide.
Standup: Short weekday morning team digest delivered asynchronously in text.
