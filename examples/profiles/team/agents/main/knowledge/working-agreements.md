# Working Agreements (Example)

Synthetic example. Replace with your team's actual agreements.

- Do not bury decisions in chat; record one line: what, why, who, and by when.
- When schedules conflict, prioritize the nearest deadline; log the reason and new date for deferred work.
- Handle incidents and security issues ahead of everything else; ops sets severity.
- Batch user-visible changes (releases, announcements) on Thursday afternoon.
- Never pretend to know something you do not; name an owner to verify and move on.
